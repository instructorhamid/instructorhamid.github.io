About me

I'm Hamid. I connect the tools your marketing already uses.

I'm a Digital Marketing & Marketing Automation Strategist based in Sylhet, Bangladesh, working remotely with businesses in the US, UK, Europe and Australia.

Most businesses I meet already have decent tools. What's missing is the connection between them. The landing page doesn't feed the CRM properly, follow-up depends on someone remembering, and nobody can say where a lead came from.

My job is to map that journey and build the system behind it: pages, pipelines, email sequences and integrations in GoHighLevel, HubSpot, n8n and Zapier. My team handles social media marketing, outreach and management, so campaigns and systems move together. Paid advertising and SEO come in when the project needs them.

Why work with me

Systems, not scattered tasks.

A landing page on its own doesn't follow up. A CRM on its own doesn't fill itself. The value comes from how the pieces are wired together.

Connected system

Every tool hands off to the next. Leads arrive tagged with their source, get the right emails and land in front of your team at the right moment.

  • Connected, not isolated

    Landing pages, CRM, emails and team alerts are planned as one journey, so a lead never falls between tools.

  • Practical implementation

    I build and configure the real thing: the pages, pipelines, workflows and email sequences. You get a working system, not just a strategy deck.

  • Organized operations

    Clean pipelines, consistent tags and custom fields, and documentation your team can follow after handover.

  • The right tool for the job

    GoHighLevel, HubSpot, n8n or Zapier, chosen to fit your process and budget instead of forcing your process to fit a tool.

  • Built around the customer

    Each automation maps to a moment your customer actually goes through: registering, attending, booking or buying.

  • Clear, tested, improved

    You always know what's being built and why. Every path is tested before real leads reach it, then refined from real data.

The process I work

Six steps, one loop.

Each stage feeds the next, and the last one feeds the first. A marketing system is never really finished; it gets measured and improved as the business grows.

The loop closes at stage six: what you measure becomes the starting brief for the next round of improvements.

  1. 1

    Understand the business

    We start with your goals, offer, audience and the tools you already pay for. I want to know what a good lead looks like and where things currently break.

    Goals & requirements brief
  2. 2

    Map the journey

    I map every step a customer takes, from first click to booked call or purchase, alongside your current workflow, so gaps and manual steps become visible.

    Customer journey map
  3. 3

    Design the system

    I plan the pages, pipeline stages, tags, email sequences and automations the journey needs, and choose which tools handle each part.

    System blueprint
  4. 4

    Build pages, CRM & automation

    Landing pages, forms, pipelines, workflows and emails get built. Where social media or ads are part of the plan, my team runs those in step with the build.

    Working pages & workflows
  5. 5

    Connect & test

    Tools are integrated and every path is tested end to end: forms, tags, triggers, emails, bookings and alerts, before a real lead touches it.

    Tested integrations
  6. 6

    Measure & improve

    Once live, we track how leads move through the system and refine the weak points. Those insights become the brief for the next cycle.

    Back to step 1 with real data

Fleet To Glory Ltd.Full-funnel marketing system in GoHighLevel · via Drogon Consulting

A system that captures paid ad traffic, qualifies leads automatically and runs event-driven nurture sequences without manual touchpoints.

  • Ad-to-CRM tracking. Webhook pipelines capture Meta and Google leads with UTM source, campaign and medium mapped to custom fields; GA4 and Conversions API events configured.
  • Compliant funnel. Multi-step lead capture, verification and upsell checkout, with A2P 10DLC consent, opt-out terms and real-time contact validation.
  • Event-driven workflows. Conditional branches and countdown waits timed to live events, routing contacts by registration, link clicks or inactivity.
  • Pipeline & deliverability. Automatic stage updates, lead scoring, deduplication and team alerts; a separate sending subdomain authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
