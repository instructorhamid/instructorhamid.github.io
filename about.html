My story

I'm Hamid. I connect the tools your marketing already uses.

I'm a Digital Marketing & Marketing Automation Strategist based in Sylhet, Bangladesh, working remotely with businesses in the US, UK, Europe and Australia.

Most businesses I meet already have decent tools. What's missing is the connection between them. The landing page doesn't feed the CRM properly, follow-up depends on someone remembering, and nobody can say where a lead came from.

My job is to map that journey and build the system behind it: pages, pipelines, email sequences and integrations in GoHighLevel, HubSpot, n8n and Zapier. My team handles social media marketing, outreach and management, so campaigns and systems move together. Paid advertising and SEO come in when the project needs them.

I studied Computer Technology at Sylhet Polytechnic Institute, and since 2022 I've trained aspiring marketers at FreelancerLab across 15+ batches. Teaching is why I explain technical work in plain language.
