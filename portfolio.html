Fleet To Glory Ltd.Full-funnel marketing system in GoHighLevel · via Drogon Consulting

A system that captures paid ad traffic, qualifies leads automatically and runs event-driven nurture sequences without manual touchpoints.

  • Ad-to-CRM tracking. Webhook pipelines capture Meta and Google leads with UTM source, campaign and medium mapped to custom fields; GA4 and Conversions API events configured.
  • Compliant funnel. Multi-step lead capture, verification and upsell checkout, with A2P 10DLC consent, opt-out terms and real-time contact validation.
  • Event-driven workflows. Conditional branches and countdown waits timed to live events, routing contacts by registration, link clicks or inactivity.
  • Pipeline & deliverability. Automatic stage updates, lead scoring, deduplication and team alerts; a separate sending subdomain authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.

Landing pageSep 2026

kamalikumanan.comWorkshop registration page for life coach Kamali Kumanan · via Drogon Consulting

The front door of a paid social funnel. Cold Instagram and Facebook traffic lands here and makes one decision: reserve a seat at a free live workshop, or leave.

  • One promise, no exits. No navigation, no outbound links, no secondary offer. The CTA repeats four times down the page.
  • A specific hook. The headline speaks to people who've already tried vision boards and affirmations, a warmer audience than "anyone interested in abundance."
  • Objections in order. Promise, mechanism, takeaways, credibility, testimonials, logistics, close.
  • Mobile first. Headline, date and button fit the first phone screen.
  • Automated follow-through. A registration workflow tags each contact, sends a Meta Conversions API event, delivers the confirmation email and runs timed nurture emails up to the workshop.
